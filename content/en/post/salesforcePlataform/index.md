# What is Salesforce?

A short answer for what is Salesforce, it is a CRM(Customer Relationship Management) , that runs on the cloud. The main point is to help a company to interact with current and potential clients. Salesforce started ad 1999 and has grow a lot to help the companies to manage their relation with clients. In order to keep it importance and relevance Salesforce updated, released new products and bought others during the years. So I will separate Salesforce Products in core platform and not. The main advantage of core platform product is that the integration is easy between core products (they have access to the Standard and Custom Objects) and you can use Salesforce Platforms tools as flow, lightning page, Mlesoft composer and surveys. The Salesforce has 16 products:

+ Sales
+ Service
+ Marketing
+ Commerce
+ Tableau
+ Mulesoft
+ Slack
+ Platform
+ Net Zero
+ Industries
+ Success
